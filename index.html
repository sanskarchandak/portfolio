About Me

I'm a QA Engineer with 4 years of experience in functional, manual, API, and integration testing across financial systems and order management platforms. I have hands-on exposure to test automation using Selenium WebDriver, Core Java, TestNG, Maven, and Page Object Model, along with REST API testing using Postman and Swagger and SQL-based backend validation.

Currently at Cognizant Technology Services, I've worked with global clients including Ernst & Young and Mattel, validating end-to-end business workflows across financial audit, order management, CRM, and warehouse management systems. I collaborate with business analysts, developers, and cross-functional teams to analyze requirements, design test scenarios, investigate defects, and support successful releases.

Alongside my professional QA experience, I continue to strengthen my test automation and CI/CD skills through hands-on projects. My current focus includes building automation frameworks with Selenium, Java, TestNG, and Maven, while gaining practical experience with Jenkins, Git, GitHub, and CI/CD workflows.
